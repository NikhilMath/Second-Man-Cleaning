Add it to Gmail

  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "Second Man Cleaning".
  3. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for both For new emails and On reply/forward.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
