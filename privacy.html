Privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Who we are

Second Man Cleaning is a commercial cleaning company in Logan, Utah. Questions about this policy go to office@secondmancleaning.com or (435) 232-8058.

What we collect

The instant estimate runs in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you book a walkthrough. When you do, we receive:

  • Your business name, your name, title, phone number and email
  • The facility address
  • Your preferred walkthrough date, time window and service start date
  • Your estimate answers (size, facility type, frequency and so on) and any notes you write

Your estimate answers are also saved in the page address, so a copied share link includes them. Anyone you send that link to can see those answers.

How we use it

We use your information only to contact you, schedule and carry out your walkthrough, prepare your quote and provide service if you hire us. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it goes

Booking requests are handled by our website host, Netlify, and delivered to our business email. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

Email us any time to ask what information we have about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
