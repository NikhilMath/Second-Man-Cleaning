FAQ

Questions, answered

What kinds of businesses do you clean?

Offices, retail stores, clinics, studios, gyms and other commercial spaces. If it's a workplace, we can likely help.

Do you clean during business hours?

We usually clean before opening or after closing so we don't disrupt your staff or customers, but we can work whatever schedule suits you.

Do you provide supplies?

Yes. We bring all cleaning supplies and equipment. We can also restock restroom paper products and soap.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on the size of your space and how often we clean. After a walkthrough, you'll get a flat monthly quote.

Are you insured?

Yes. We're fully insured and bonded, and every team member is background-checked.
