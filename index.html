FAQ

Commercial cleaning in Logan: common questions

Do you offer commercial cleaning in Logan, Utah?

Yes. Second Man Cleaning provides commercial cleaning for businesses in Logan and throughout Cache Valley, including North Logan, Hyde Park, Smithfield, Providence, River Heights, Nibley, Millville and Hyrum.

What does your commercial cleaning include?

Trash removal with new liners, restroom cleaning and sanitizing with soap and paper restocked, plus vacuuming, mopping, dusting and wiping down offices and work areas. If your business needs more than that, we're open to adding it. Just tell us at your walkthrough or in the booking notes, and we'll build it into your plan.

When do you clean?

After hours, so your team and customers are never interrupted and your workplace is ready when you open.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Second Man Cleaning is licensed and insured, and veteran owned and operated. Our certificate of insurance is available on request: just ask, or tick the box when you book, and we'll email it before your walkthrough or first clean.

How do I get a quote?

Use our instant estimate to see a monthly price range in under 2 minutes, then book a free walkthrough for your final quote. You can also call (435) 232-8058 or email office@secondmancleaning.com. Quotes are always free.
