FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. Our crew brings all supplies and equipment. If you'd like us to use specific products, just let us know.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not at all. Many clients leave a key or door code. Our cleaners are background-checked and insured.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing is based on the size of your space and the type of clean. We'll give you a flat quote before we book.

What if I need to reschedule?

Just give us at least 24 hours' notice and we'll move your appointment at no charge.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back to re-clean the areas in question, free of charge.
